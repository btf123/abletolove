# Founder Voice Guide: writing as Able2Love / Brogan Fox

This guide governs every public word the campaign produces, human or machine-written. It is built from Brogan's own style preferences. It contains no private information and is safe to keep in the repo.

## Who is talking

Able2Love speaks in the voice of its founder: a Manchester-based comedy writer and performer who is a full-time wheelchair user. Performer first. The wheelchair is part of the picture, never the whole picture.

## What the app is (say it this way)

Able2Love connects **disabled and non-disabled people who are open to dating one another**, in a place where visible disability is normalised rather than hidden or fetishised. It is about ordinary attraction, choice, confidence and mutual interest. Not vulnerability. Not charity. Dating.

## Hard rules

1. **UK English.** Normalise, colour, maths, favourite. Dates and currency in UK format.
2. **No em dashes. Ever.** Use commas, brackets, colons, semicolons or full stops instead.
3. **No AI-cliché words:** passionate, journey, empowering, inspiring, unique, transformative. If one appears, rewrite the sentence.
4. **No pity, no inspiration-porn, no "despite".** Already banned language stays banned: suffers from, wheelchair-bound, special needs, differently abled, brave, overcame.
5. **Direct beats padded.** If a sentence works without a word, cut the word.
6. **Funny is allowed and encouraged.** The founder studied comedy. Dry, blunt, observational humour fits the brand. Dark is fine; cruel is not.
7. **Emotionally honest when it matters.** Serious posts should sound like a person, not a press office.

## Voice reference: rewrite examples

| Too corporate / wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| "We're passionate about empowering the disability community on their dating journey" | "Dating apps weren't built for us, so we built one" |
| "Able2Love is a unique, transformative platform" | "It's a dating app that doesn't make you explain yourself" |
| "She found love despite her disability" | Never. Delete. Start again. |
| "Join our inspiring community" | "Come find your people" |

## Founder bio (public-safe, for press kit and about pages)

> Able2Love was founded by Brogan Fox, a Manchester-based comedy writer, performer and producer, and a University of Salford Comedy Writing and Performance graduate. A full-time wheelchair user, Brogan made his name with the first-class autobiographical show *Sitting Right Here*, about how audiences see the wheelchair before the person. Able2Love is that argument turned into a product: dating where disabled and non-disabled people meet as people first.
