## Boilerplate (paste into any article)
Able2Love is a dating app for disabled and non-disabled people who are open to dating one another. Founded by Brogan Fox, a Greater Manchester comedy writer and performer who is a full-time wheelchair user, it puts access and disclosure on the user's own terms: your access needs live on your profile as tap-to-reveal cards rather than a speech you rehearse in the DMs, an accessible-venue planner lets matches pick step-free places and share them in chat, and a "See Me First" feature keeps extra photos blurred until a conversation gets going. Able2Love is free on Google Play.

## Founder bio
Brogan Fox is a comedy writer, performer, musician and producer from Greater Manchester, and a University of Salford Comedy Writing and Performance graduate. A full-time wheelchair user, he built Able2Love after years of mainstream dating apps that treated disability as an afterthought. Performer first, founder second: the wheelchair is part of the picture, never the whole picture.

## Why it exists (the story)
Disability is too often allowed to define someone before their humour, personality, attraction or ambition gets a look in. On mainstream apps that shows up as "the disclosure dread": the drafted-and-deleted message, the third-date reveal, the wondering whether to mention it at all. Able2Love removes that by making access and disclosure ordinary and up front, for disabled and non-disabled people alike. It is not a charity, a support service, or a fetish platform. It is a dating app that takes access seriously.

## Key features (accurate, no embellishment)
- **Disclosure cards:** access needs and disability info as optional, tap-to-reveal cards on your profile.
- **Plan our date:** browse step-free, accessible venues and share them with a match in chat.
- **See Me First:** extra photos start blurred and come into focus as a conversation grows.
- **Nearby:** a map of people nearby and accessible venues, with locations roughly placed for privacy.
- Free on Google Play (Android).
