# The Able2Love Outreach Program

The goal: household name. The method: every day, show up where the community already is, in the founder's real voice, and put the app in front of the people who can amplify it. UK first, worldwide always on.

**How it runs:** a robot (`automation/generate-outreach.mjs`) hunts every morning: fresh conversations worth joining, a daily outreach target, funding calls, trending moments. It drafts personality-voiced comments and DMs, and files a brief. A human (you, or the Claude extension under your review) sends them. The robot never posts by itself; auto-posting replies is how accounts get banned and how a voice goes off-brand. Hunt automated, hand human.

## 2. The daily rhythm (robot hunts, human sends)

Every morning the brief lands with:
1. **8 to 12 fresh conversations** found in the last 24 hours (posts, threads, news) across the community, UK-weighted but global, each with a drafted comment in the founder's voice.
2. **One outreach target of the day** from the directory rota, with a drafted DM or reply that fits what they posted this week.
3. **Funding and opportunity watch:** any open calls, deadlines, press requests (#JournoRequest), award windows.
4. **Moment watch:** anything trending the brand can join honestly (awareness days, viral threads about dating apps, access fails in the news).

Comment style (this is the personality contract):
- A real reaction first: agree, laugh, add an experience, answer the question.
- The app enters only when it belongs: "this exact thing is why I built a dating app with the access info up front" — and often it shouldn't enter at all.
- NEVER: "check out my app", "download my app", link-dropping in strangers' comments, or compliment-then-plug.
- UK English, no em dashes, no pity framing, no invented facts.

## 3. Money and support (the founder-support radar)

Live leads found so far (verify dates on gov/charity pages when applying):
- **UnLtd** — £500 to £15,000 awards for early social entrepreneurs, rolling. **Best immediate fit.** unltd.org.uk
- **Stelios Awards for Disabled Entrepreneurs** (Leonard Cheshire + Stelios Foundation) — £150k top prize, annual, applications roughly March to April. 2026 is closed; needs about £200k turnover, so it is the *grow-into-it* target for 2027/28.
- **Access to Work** (gov.uk) — funds support costs for disabled people running a business (equipment, support workers, travel). Applies to you personally as founder.
- **Innovate UK / local growth hubs** — tech grants; GM Business Growth Hub for Manchester specifically.
- **disability-grants.org** and **disabledentrepreneurs.co.uk** — directories the robot checks for new schemes.

The daily brief includes a funding-watch section; the weekly review issue rolls up anything with a deadline.

## 4. What you didn't ask for but need (the missing pieces)

- **Short video is the real engine.** You are a performer; a 20 second piece to camera outperforms every static card. One reel a week minimum: why I built it, the disclosure-card demo, the "ringing ahead" bit. This is the single biggest reach unlock available and only you can record it.
- **Your performer accounts are the launchpad.** The brand account is cold; your own audience isn't. Share every milestone from your personal accounts.
- **BBC Access All + local press before anything paid.** "Manchester comedian builds the dating app disabled people actually asked for" is a story editors take. The press kit in `05-aso-and-press-kit.md` is ready.
- **App store reviews are marketing.** Every happy tester should be asked to leave a Play Store review; ratings drive the store algorithm more than posts do.
- **Awareness-day calendar:** the robot flags them (Disability Pride Month is July, right now; International Day of Persons with Disabilities 3 Dec; Deaf Awareness Week; Purple Tuesday). Post and engage harder on those days.
- **Partnership beats promotion:** Euan's Guide/AccessAble tie-in, a comedy-night launch event in an accessible Manchester venue (performer + product in one story), guest slots instead of adverts.
- **Product Hunt / Indie Hackers launch** for the tech audience wave once See Me First is fixed.

## 5. Red lines (so growth never kills the account)

- No auto-posted replies or DMs. Drafts always pass a human.
- No mass-tagging, no follow/unfollow churn, no DM blasts to strangers (on a dating app account this reads predatory).
- No engagement on fetish/objectifying content or tags, ever, regardless of reach.
- No invented stats, features or momentum in any outreach message.
- If a creator says no or ignores two approaches, they come off the rota for a quarter.
