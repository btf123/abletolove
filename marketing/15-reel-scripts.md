# Reel / TikTok scripts — ready to film

Short video is the single biggest reach lever we have, and it's the one thing only you can do. You're a performer; this is home turf. Each of these is 15 to 35 seconds, written in your voice: dry, Manchester, no pity, no inspiration guff. Film them to camera, one take, phone is fine. Post to Instagram Reels and TikTok, and drop the audio-free ones to X as video too.

Rules baked in: UK English, no invented stats, disability is never the punchline, the app plug is light. Pick the ones that feel like you and change any word that doesn't.

---

## 1. The plot twist
**On camera, deadpan.**
"On every other dating app, my disability is a plot twist. There's a whole nervous speech: 'so, there's something I should tell you.' Mate, it's a wheelchair, not a criminal record. So I built an app where it's just on my profile from the start. Able2Love. Free on Google Play."
**On-screen text:** "Not a plot twist. Just a profile."

## 2. The bio cliches
**Reading imaginary bios off your phone.**
"'Love to laugh.' As opposed to what. 'Love to travel.' A personality, or an airport? 'No drama.' Says the person about to be so much drama. Green flags only over here. Able2Love."
**On-screen text:** "Write a better bio."

## 3. Ringing ahead
"The least romantic part of dating with a wheelchair? Ringing the restaurant beforehand. 'Hi, do you have a step? A toilet I can actually use? A door wider than a letterbox?' Very sexy. So the app just tells you which places are accessible and you send it to your date. Revolutionary, I know. Water is also wet."
**On-screen text:** "Plan our date · step-free spots, sorted."

## 4. Not your good deed
**Straight to camera, warm but firm.**
"Quick reminder for the people in the back: disabled people are not on dating apps to be your good deed. We're there for the same reasons as you. Someone funny. Someone fit. Someone who texts back within the same calendar month. That's it. That's the video."
**On-screen text:** "Same reasons as everyone."

## 5. See Me First
"Hot take: you should have to earn the second photo. On Able2Love, someone's other pictures start blurred and come into focus the more you actually talk. Radical concept: getting to know a person before you've fully clocked their jawline."
**On-screen text:** "The more you talk, the more you see."

## 6. The questions people ask
**Counting on fingers.**
"Things strangers have asked me on dates: 'Can you feel your legs?' 'How do you, you know...?' 'What happened to you?' Things a good date asks: 'What are you drinking?' Built an app that attracts more of the second one."
**On-screen text:** "Ask better questions."

## 7. Founder origin
"I'm a comedian from Manchester and a wheelchair user, and I got sick of dating apps built like disabled people don't date. So I made one. It's called Able2Love, it's free, it's on Google Play, and no, this isn't a charity thing, it's a fancy-someone thing."
**On-screen text:** "Made in Manchester."

## 8. The disclosure speech no one drafts
"There's a text every disabled person on a dating app has drafted and deleted forty times. The 'so there's something you should know' one. On Able2Love you don't write it. It's a card on your profile. Tap to read. First message gets to be a normal one for once."
**On-screen text:** "The speech no one here has to write."

## 9. Accessible venue rant (comedy)
"Restaurants love an accessible toilet that you get to by going out the front, round the back, past the bins, through the kitchen, and asking Dave for a key. That's not access, that's an escape room. The app just shows you the genuinely step-free ones. Wild."
**On-screen text:** "Access, not an escape room."

## 10. Two truths
"Two things are true. Disabled people are hilarious, complicated, fit, and fully capable of ghosting you. And most dating apps were not built with any of us in mind. Able2Love fixes the second one. The ghosting is on you."
**On-screen text:** "We contain multitudes. And read receipts."

## 11. For the non-disabled folks
"If you're not disabled and you're wondering if this app is for you too: yes. It's for disabled and non-disabled people who are open to dating each other. You don't get a medal for it. You just get a date. That's the whole pitch."
**On-screen text:** "Everyone's welcome. No medals."

## 12. The map
"There's a map on the app that shows two things at once: people near you, and cafes and bars you can actually get into. Tap one filter and the step-free places light up green. I nearly cried. It's a map. I need to get out more, which, conveniently, the map also helps with."
**On-screen text:** "People near you. Places you can get into."

## 13. What it is not
"Able2Love is not a charity. Not a support group. Not a fetish thing, put your phone down. It's a dating app. Disabled and non-disabled people, fancying each other, like adults. Shocking, I know."
**On-screen text:** "It's just a dating app. That's the point."

## 14. Disability Pride Month
"It's Disability Pride Month, so here's some pride: I built a dating app. Me. The one your nan worries about. It's live, it's free, and it's for anyone who thinks disabled people deserve a decent night out and someone to share it with. Able2Love."
**On-screen text:** "Proud. And on Google Play."

## 15. The soft one
**Quieter, still dry.**
"If you've ever put your phone down after another app conversation went sideways the second you mentioned your disability, this is the bit where I tell you there's another option. It's called Able2Love. It's free. And the awkward part is already handled. That's all."
**On-screen text:** "You belong here. Free on Google Play."

## Filming notes
- One take, phone at eye level, decent light on your face, quiet room. Authentic beats polished.
- Say the app name and "free on Google Play" once, near the end, not the start.
- Put the on-screen text up as a caption; it doubles your reach for people watching on mute.
- Add the same hashtags as your posts (`#disabilitydating #disabilitycommunity #datingwithadisability #able2love`), 3 to 5 max.
- Link in bio does the work; never put the store link in the video itself.
- One a week beats seven then silence. Bank a few on a good day.
