# Campaign Strategy — "You Belong Here"

## The product in one line
Able To Love is a dating app built with the disability community, where sharing how you move through the world is a normal part of your profile — not an awkward reveal on the third date.

## Why now
- ~1 in 4 adults lives with a disability, yet mainstream dating apps treat disability as an edge case or something to hide.
- The most common pain point disabled daters report is **the disclosure moment** — deciding when and how to tell a match. Able To Love removes that anxiety by making disclosure a first-class, optional, opt-in profile field surrounded by people who already get it.
- Accessibility isn't a feature bolted on; it's the reason the app exists. That is a defensible, authentic wedge no incumbent can copy without rebuilding their culture.

## Positioning
**For** disabled adults and the people who love them
**Who** are tired of hiding, over-explaining, or being fetishized on generic dating apps,
**Able To Love is** a dating app where your disability is context, not a caveat,
**Unlike** Tinder/Hinge/Bumble that treat accessibility as an afterthought,
**We** built the whole experience — profiles, filters, chat, and community norms — around the community, with the community.

## Audience segments
1. **Primary — Disabled daters (22–45).** Physical, sensory, chronic-illness, neurodivergent. Active on Instagram, TikTok, Reddit (r/disability, condition-specific subs), and disability creator ecosystems.
2. **Secondary — Allies & partners.** People who date disabled partners and want a space without stigma.
3. **Amplifiers — Disability creators & advocates.** Micro-influencers (10k–200k) whose audiences trust them. This is the single highest-leverage channel; see `04`.
4. **Institutional — Advocacy orgs, accessible-living communities, adaptive-sports groups.** Slow but high-trust partnerships.

## Goals & funnel
| Stage | Metric | 90-day target |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Impressions across owned + creator channels | 2.0M |
| Interest | Landing-page waitlist signups | 15,000 |
| Activation | App installs → completed profile | 40% of installs |
| Retention | D30 retention | 25% |
| Referral | % of new users from a member invite | 20% |

Waitlist-first: we drive to the landing page (`landing-page/index.html`) before store launch to build a warm list, then convert the list on launch day for a concentrated ranking spike.

## Channel mix & priority
1. **Creator partnerships** (highest ROI) — authentic testimonials from disability creators.
2. **Organic short-form** (TikTok + Reels) — relatable, funny, community-in-jokes; not ads.
3. **Community seeding** — Reddit, Discord, Facebook groups. Show up as a member, not a billboard.
4. **Waitlist landing page + referral loop** — every signup can invite friends up the queue.
5. **PR / earned media** — accessibility and inclusion angle pitched to lifestyle + disability press (see `05`).
6. **Paid** (later, once creative is validated) — Meta/TikTok retargeting to landing-page visitors only, low budget.

## Indicative budget (first 90 days, lean)
| Line | Range |
|---|---|
| Creator partnerships (10–15 micro-creators) | $6,000–12,000 |
| Paid retargeting (validated creative only) | $3,000–5,000 |
| Landing page hosting + email tool | $0–100/mo |
| Automation infra (social-media-bot) | ~$30–60/mo (see its README) |
| Design/asset production | $1,000–2,000 |
| **Total** | **~$10k–19k** |

Everything above is achievable at a fraction of this by leaning on organic + the automation engine already in the repo; paid is optional accelerant, not the engine.

## What we will NOT do
- No inspiration-porn ("she found love *despite* her disability").
- No fetishization or "devotee"-adjacent messaging.
- No fake profiles, engagement pods, or bought followers — trust is the product.
- No scraping or DMing people's private accounts without consent.
- No pity. Ever.
