No small print

So what's the catch?

People keep asking me what the catch is. Fair enough. There isn't one, but I know that answer sounds like every scam ever, so let me actually explain.

Why is it free?

Because I pay for it, out of my own pocket, before the app has ever made a penny. One day there might be optional extras for people who want them, and that would keep the lights on. But the app you have now is free, because charging lonely people to stop being lonely never sat right with me, and because the community this is for has been overcharged by life already.

Are you selling my data?

No. Your data is not the product, because there is no product being sold to anyone. Your profile lives in a locked database where only you and the people you choose to show can see what you share. Everything travels encrypted. I do not sell it, rent it, or share it with advertisers. If that ever changed I would have to tell you first.

Is anything reading my messages?

No. The kindness filter people ask about is a simple list of hurtful words that lives on your phone. When it spots one, it asks if you are sure before you send. Your messages stay between you and the person you sent them to.

What about the voice features?

The voice you hear reading profiles out loud is your own phone's built in voice, the same one that reads your text messages. Nobody is listening and nothing is recorded.

So what do you get out of it, Brogan?

I get to watch people like me be wanted for who they are. That is the whole business model. I read every piece of feedback myself, I fix what people tell me is broken, and the people who were here at the start have a founding member badge on their profile, because they built this with me.

Questions

The honest FAQ.

Is it free?

Yes. Free to download on Google Play.

Is it only for disabled people?

No. It's for disabled and non-disabled people who are open to dating one another. Everyone's welcome, no medals for turning up.

Do I have to share my disability?

Only if you want to. Disclosure is optional and opt-in. You choose what goes on your profile, and you can change or remove it any time.

What phones does it work on?

It's on Android via Google Play right now.

Is my location private?

Yes. On the Nearby map, locations are deliberately fuzzed to roughly 100m, so nobody sees an exact address, and only people active right now appear.

How do I stay safe and report someone?

You can report and block other users in the app. As with any dating app, meet in public first and tell someone where you're going. See our safety tips.
