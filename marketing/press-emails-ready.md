# Able2Love — press emails, ready to send

Four emails, each tailored to the outlet. Send them one at a time (a personal,
single email lands far better than an obvious blast).

**Two things to do before sending, both quick:**
1. Swap `[your contact email]` for the address you want press to reply to. If you
   don't have a support@ address yet, your own email is fine.
2. **Confirm the "To:" address** on each — outlets change contacts, so give it a
   10-second check on their website before you hit send. Best-known addresses are
   noted, but verify.

Nothing in these makes up numbers, funding, or awards. Keep it that way if you edit.

Hi Access All team,

I'm Brogan Fox, a comedy writer and wheelchair user in Manchester. After years of mainstream dating apps treating a wheelchair like a warning label, all the right words and then a vanishing act, I built the app they never would: Able2Love, a dating app for disabled and non-disabled people, with accessibility built in rather than bolted on.
