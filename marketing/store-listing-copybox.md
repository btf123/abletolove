## BOX 3 — Full description  (field: "Full description", max 4000 chars)

```
Able2Love is a dating app for disabled and non-disabled people who are open to dating each other. Inclusive by design, with accessibility built in from the start, not bolted on afterwards.

Built in Manchester by someone who got sick of mainstream dating apps treating a wheelchair like a warning label. Able2Love is for anyone who wants a real connection without the awkward explanations, the ghosting, or the "it's not you, it's me".

WHY ABLE2LOVE
- Made for disabled daters and the people who want to date them. Visible or invisible disability, chronic illness, wheelchair user, Deaf, neurodivergent, spoonie: you belong here.
- Access first. Your needs live on your profile, on your terms, so it is not a conversation you have to keep having.
- Same reasons as everyone else: someone funny, someone fit, someone who actually texts back.

FEATURES
- Disclosure cards: share your access needs or disability as tap-to-reveal cards on your profile. Set it out once, your way, instead of explaining it in every chat.
- Plan our date: browse step-free, accessible venues and share them with your match, so you plan somewhere you can both actually get into.
- See Me First: on some profiles the extra photos start blurred and come into focus as you chat, so people connect on conversation first.
- Nearby: a map of people and accessible venues near you. Locations are fuzzed for privacy, and only people active right now appear.
- Messaging and video chat with your matches.

Free to download. No catch.

Dating should not come with a door policy. Download Able2Love and find your people.
```

## After you save

- **Screenshots** lift installs more than almost anything else. If you can,
  caption each one with the feature it shows (Disclosure cards, Plan our date,
  Nearby). Captions aren't indexed, but they convert.
- Google can take a few hours to a day or two to re-index the new title. Give it
  that before judging whether search visibility moved.
