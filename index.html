Why Able2Love

Mainstream apps treat disability as an edge case. We made it the starting point.

Disclosure on your terms

Sharing about your disability is optional and opt-in — you decide what and when, among people who already get it.

Accessible by design

Screen-reader friendly, high-contrast, large touch targets. Accessibility is the foundation, not a settings toggle.

Built with, not for

Designed alongside disabled users. A community of daters, allies, and partners — without the stigma.

Made for connection

Swipe, match, and chat. Find people nearby on the map. The fun, hopeful, human parts of dating.
