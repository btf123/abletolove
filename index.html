Talk To Me

Put your phone down. The app introduces people out loud, and you browse, like and match entirely by voice — the first dating experience you can use without looking at the screen.

Blind Date

A voice call with someone compatible, photos locked and no names, until you both choose to keep talking. A first date, not a slot machine.

The Feed

Say your piece on the day's talking points and meet whoever makes you smile. You match over a sense of humour, not a selfie.

Here Now

Same train, same café — see who's genuinely around you, only when you choose to be found. Green light means come say hi.

In any call, type and the app speaks your words out loud — so someone who can't use their voice can still go on a voice date. As far as we know, a first.

Questions

The honest FAQ.

Is it free?

Yes. Free to download on Google Play.

Is it only for disabled people?

No. It's for disabled and non-disabled people who are open to dating one another. Everyone's welcome, no medals for turning up.

Do I have to share my disability?

Only if you want to. Disclosure is optional and opt-in. You choose what goes on your profile, and you can change or remove it any time.

What phones does it work on?

It's on Android via Google Play right now.

Is my location private?

Yes. On the Nearby map, locations are deliberately fuzzed to roughly 100m, so nobody sees an exact address, and only people active right now appear.

How do I stay safe and report someone?

You can report and block other users in the app. As with any dating app, meet in public first and tell someone where you're going. See our safety tips.
