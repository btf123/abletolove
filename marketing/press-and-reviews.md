# Able2Love — reviews push + press pitch

Two of the fastest, free ways to lift downloads for a new app: early reviews
(social proof + ranking) and press (a burst of the right eyeballs). Neither
costs money.

---

## 1. Reviews / ratings push

Aim: the first 10 to 20 genuine Google Play ratings. Below ~10 reviews the app
looks untested; past that it looks real, which lifts both search ranking and the
install rate.

How to get them (no incentives, that breaks Play policy):
- Ask your existing users directly. A story/post and a pinned message:
  "If Able2Love has been good to you, a quick rating on Google Play genuinely
  helps other disabled daters find it. Takes ten seconds:
  https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.abletolove.app"
- Ask friends/family who downloaded at launch to leave an honest rating.
- When someone says something nice in a DM or comment, reply warmly and add a
  soft "a rating on the Play Store helps more people find us, if you fancy it".
- Never offer anything in return for a review. Never fake reviews. Both get the
  listing penalised.

Respond to every review, good or bad, in your voice. Replying to reviews is a
ranking signal and looks human.

---

## 2. Press pitch (the founder story)

The hook is strong and true: a Manchester disabled comedy writer, sick of being
treated like a warning label on the mainstream apps, built the dating app they
refused to make. That is a story disability and local media actually want.

### Target outlets and people
- BBC Access All (disability podcast) — access-all@bbc.co.uk style pitch
- Disability Horizons (online magazine)
- PosAbility Magazine
- The Unwritten / disability blogs and newsletters
- Metro (has strong disability coverage)
- Manchester Evening News (local angle: built in Manchester)
- Euan's Guide (accessible-venue community, natural partner for Plan our date)
- Disabled creators on Instagram/TikTok (interabled couples, disability + dating)

### Email template
