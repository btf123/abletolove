## Full description  (max 4000 characters)

Able2Love is a dating app for disabled and non-disabled people who are open to
dating each other. Inclusive by design, with accessibility built in from the
start, not bolted on afterwards.

Built in Manchester by someone who got sick of mainstream dating apps treating a
wheelchair like a warning label. Able2Love is for anyone who wants a real
connection without the awkward explanations, the ghosting, or the "it's not you,
it's me".

WHY ABLE2LOVE
- Made for disabled daters and the people who want to date them. Visible or
  invisible disability, chronic illness, wheelchair user, Deaf, neurodivergent,
  spoonie: you belong here.
- Access first. Your needs live on your profile, on your terms, so it is not a
  conversation you have to keep having.
- Same reasons as everyone else: someone funny, someone fit, someone who
  actually texts back.

FEATURES
- Disclosure cards: share your access needs or disability as tap-to-reveal cards
  on your profile. Set it out once, your way, instead of explaining it in every
  chat.
- Plan our date: browse step-free, accessible venues and share them with your
  match, so you plan somewhere you can both actually get into.
- See Me First: on some profiles the extra photos start blurred and come into
  focus as you chat, so people connect on conversation first.
- Nearby: a map of people and accessible venues near you. Locations are fuzzed
  for privacy, and only people active right now appear.
- Messaging and video chat with your matches.

Free to download. No catch.

Dating should not come with a door policy. Download Able2Love and find your
people.

Tips:
- Repeat the top 2-3 keywords naturally a few times across the full description
  (Google indexes it). Do not stuff.
- Keep the first two lines strong: they show above the fold.
- Update screenshots to show the real features (disclosure cards, Plan our date,
  Nearby) with a short caption on each. Captions are read by users, not indexed,
  but lift conversion a lot.
