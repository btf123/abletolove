# 30-Day Launch Content Calendar

Structured around a **waitlist-first launch**: build a warm list for ~3 weeks, then convert it on launch day for a concentrated install spike.

**Legend:** IG = Instagram · TT = TikTok · X = Twitter/X · YT = YouTube Shorts · RD = Reddit/communities · EM = email to waitlist

## Weekly themes
- **Week 1 — Arrival:** "You belong here." Introduce the app and the disclosure-dread problem.
- **Week 2 — Belonging:** Community stories, the "built with, not for" pillar, creator collabs go live.
- **Week 3 — Momentum:** Referral push, feature spotlights, press hits, countdown to launch.
- **Week 4 — Launch & love:** Store go-live, launch-day blitz, first-match stories, keep the flywheel spinning.

## Cadence guidance
- **TikTok/Reels:** 1–2 per day is fine if quality holds; short-form rewards volume + iteration.
- **X:** 1–3/day, mix of replies and original.
- **Instagram feed:** 4–5/week; Stories daily.
- **YouTube Shorts:** 2–3/week.
- **Email:** never more than ~2/week; every send earns the next open.

## Launch-day (Day 27) run-of-show
1. 8:00 — Email blast to full waitlist with one-tap store links.
2. 8:05 — All owned social posts go live simultaneously.
3. 8:15 — Creator partners post their pre-agreed launch content in the same window (concentrated spike helps store ranking).
4. Midday — Reply to every comment/DM in the first 6 hours; staff the flagged-reply queue.
5. Evening — Recap Story/Short with day-one numbers (round, honest).

The `social-media-bot` engine can schedule and publish this calendar; keep a human on approvals for anything community-facing.
